          The clock is the home screen. Swipe up for the board, swipe down or tap Clock at the top to go back. Set 0 to stay on the board.

          Round change

          Every hour from wake-up to lights out, the Call of Duty zombies round change plays. It starts early, so the round changes right on the hour. It stays quiet while you're out.

          Round change every hour
          Round numberOn the clock and the board. Tally marks for rounds 1 to 5, then the number, like the game.
          Game over at lights outDer Riese, and how many rounds you survived
          Round change and game over volume

          A sound file plays right on time on any iPad, with nothing on screen. The easy way: upload files named round.mp3 and gameover.mp3 next to the board on GitHub Pages, and it finds them by itself. You can also paste an MP3 link, or choose a file on this iPad. YouTube only plays when the board is opened from a website, and its player shows in the corner while it plays. Leave a box blank for the board's own sound.

          Music

          Music through the dayStarts after the alarm and stops at lights out. Quiet while you're out or meditating. The music button stops it until the next step.

          Music volume

          Night tracks are the ones with night in their name. The board learns the names the first time it plays the playlist. Your own files play instead of YouTube if you upload them next to the board: study1.mp3 onwards for studying, and day1.mp3 and night1.mp3 onwards for the rest of the day. music1.mp3 onwards, and files added here, play when nothing else can. YouTube has to show its player while it plays, so it sits in the corner. On an iPad, tap ▶︎ on it once after starting the board. After that it plays by itself. The volume sliders work on computers. On an iPad, use the side buttons.

          Display

          The board works out which iPad it's on and fits itself to the screen, the orientation and Split View.

          Week

          Each day starts with your 3:30 routine. Tap Work day, Day off or Day off + gym to fill a day from your schedule. To change one date, like a public holiday, use Day type on the Calendar. One step per line, like 17:30-19:00 Gym | Finish work and head straight to the gym. The part after | is shown on Home and read out. Tags: @mission runs sprints, @board opens Missions, @reps asks for your crunch and sit-up count, @check asks if you did it, @nocheck doesn't, @gym and @work set the day type.

          School terms

          The calendar numbers each week of term and shades the school holidays. One term per line, first and last day, like 2027-01-28 2027-04-09. Holidays are the gaps between terms. NSW public school dates for 2026 and 2027 are filled in.
